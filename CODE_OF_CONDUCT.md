# ABC 2.0 Code of Conduct

Like the technical community as a whole, the ABC 2.0 team and community are made up of a mixture of professionals and volunteers from all over the world, working on every aspect of the project—including language design, compiler engineering, documentation, mentorship, and community outreach.

Diversity is one of our greatest strengths, but it can also lead to communication challenges. To that end, we have established ground rules for community interaction. This code applies equally to project maintainers, core contributors, and new developers seeking guidance.

This isn’t an exhaustive list of forbidden actions. Rather, it is a guiding framework to make our community welcoming, productive, and enriching for all participants.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all communication spaces managed by the ABC 2.0 Project. This includes the GitHub organization (issues, pull requests, and discussions), mailing lists, community chat spaces, official events, and any other forums managed by the project team. In addition, violations of this code outside these spaces may affect a person's eligibility to participate within official project channels.

If you believe someone is violating this Code of Conduct, please report it by emailing [aran.rath@outlook.com](mailto:aran.rath@outlook.com).

---

## Community Standards

* **Be friendly and patient.**
* **Be welcoming.** We strive to be a community that welcomes and supports people of all backgrounds and identities. This includes, but is not limited to, members of any race, ethnicity, culture, national origin, color, immigration status, socioeconomic class, educational background, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, age, family status, political belief, religion, or ability.
* **Be considerate.** Your work will be used by other developers, and you will depend on the work of others. Technical decisions impact users and contributors; consider those consequences carefully. Remember that our global community includes non-native English speakers.
* **Be respectful.** Disagreements will occur, but they are no excuse for poor behavior. Frustration must not escalate into personal attacks. A community where people feel uncomfortable or unsafe is not a productive one.
* **Be careful in the words that you choose.** Conduct yourself professionally. Do not insult, demean, or harass other participants. Unacceptable behaviors include:
  * Violent threats or targeted language.
  * Discriminatory jokes and language.
  * Posting sexually explicit or violent material.
  * Posting or threatening to post private personal information ("doxxing").
  * Personal insults, especially using racist or sexist terms.
  * Unwelcome sexual attention.
  * Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors.
  * Repeated harassment. If asked to stop a behavior, comply immediately.
* **When we disagree, try to understand why.** Social and technical disagreements happen in software development. Resolve differing views constructively. Diversity of perspective strengthens the ABC 2.0 language. Focus on resolving issues and learning from mistakes rather than assigning blame.

## Questions & Reporting

If you have questions regarding this Code of Conduct or need to report an incident, please [contact the maintainers](mailto:aran.rath@outlook.com). All reports will be reviewed confidentially.
